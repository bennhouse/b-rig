b-rig, a guitar rig in one app, with its plugins for your DAW if you want them.

- **macOS (Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later)**: open the `.dmg`, then double-click "Install b-rig". The first time macOS blocks it because it is not notarized: open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". On the "Installation Type" page the app is always selected. To add the plugins for your DAW, tick "Add the plugins for your DAW" (all of them, Audio Unit and VST3), or open it to choose the format and then only the plugins you want. b-rig includes no cab IRs: load your own in b-blend to hear b-amp. The app goes to Applications, the plugins to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.
