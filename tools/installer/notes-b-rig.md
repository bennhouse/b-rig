b-rig, a guitar rig in one app, with its plugins for your DAW if you want them.

- **macOS (Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later)**: open the `.dmg`, then double-click "Install b-rig". The first time macOS blocks it because it is not notarized: open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". On the "Installation Type" page the app is always selected; "Plugin formats" chooses Audio Unit, VST3 or both for every plugin, and "Plugins for your DAW" ticks all the plugins or, opened, one by one. b-rig includes no cab IRs: load your own in b-blend to hear b-amp. The app goes to Applications, the plugins to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.
