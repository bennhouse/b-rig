b-rig, a guitar rig in one app, also as a plugin for your DAW, with the b-suite plugins if you want them.

- **macOS (Apple Silicon, macOS 11 or later)**: open the `.dmg`, then double-click "Install b-rig". The first time macOS blocks it because it is not notarized: open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". On the "Installation Type" page choose "b-rig standalone" (the app, ticked by default), "b-rig plugin" (b-rig inside your DAW) and "b-suite plugins" (every effect as its own plugin); open a line to pick only Audio Unit or VST3. b-rig includes no cab IRs: load your own in b-blend to hear b-amp. The app goes to Applications, the plugins to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins.
