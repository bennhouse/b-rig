All the b-suite plugins in one installer.

- **macOS** (Apple Silicon and Intel): open the .pkg; Audio Unit and VST3 go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins. "Customise" lets you leave out plugins. The package is not notarized: the first time, macOS blocks it. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
- **Windows x64 / ARM64**: run the setup for your machine; the VST3 plugins go to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3\b-suite. SmartScreen may warn that the publisher is unknown: click "More info" > "Run anyway".
